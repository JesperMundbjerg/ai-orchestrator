# Demo illustration provenance

The five PNGs here are original, purpose-made illustrations of fictional Lantern notes
and Pebble planner interfaces, generated by `generate.mjs` using plain HTML/CSS.
They contain no third-party screenshots, artwork, logos or downloaded fonts.
The generator and its output are covered by this repository's MIT license.

Regenerate with `node scripts/demo-assets/generate.mjs` after installing the documented
Playwright browser-test dependencies and Chromium. These are sample interfaces, not
claims about a real application's behavior.
