# Lounge games and reachable seats

The building uses `lounge.ts` for sofa positions and front approaches. `building.ts` opens the four formerly enclosed reading nooks through their neighbouring bay's front strip; the sofas face that opening and the plants stand behind them. Kitchen stools have a little more table clearance.

`seats.ts` inventories every physical seat, including unused chairs, plus occupied craft-station places. `test/games.test.ts` samples entrance routes with body clearance against walls, furniture, garden beds and other seats, in the building at several sizes. Only the final approach may enter the destination seat itself. The established building route tests remain in place too.

`games.ts` chooses two idle pool players and one darts player, retaining eligible players as the world updates. Project members use `park.ts`'s one-minute idle rule; working, done, offline, blocked, queued and waiting-on-founder agents cannot play. Creative reviews take precedence in the view. Remaining idle agents can use the lounge and reading-nook seats, or the existing garden activities.

Six pool plays and six three-dart rounds are baked once as 20 Hz frame arrays. Positions, arcs, lean/arm poses, cue movement and brief scores are lookups; runtime has no physics, random sampling or timers. Pool turns alternate sides and seeded identities pick the play. `GamePlayback` waits for actual arrival (both players for pool), then starts their common clock. Departure cancels it. `Games.tsx` draws low-poly furniture, balls, cue, darts and cached score sprites; `Avatar.tsx` uses the same clock for poses. Only active games promote the existing pacer, which still draws nothing in a hidden tab.

## Headless check

Build, then run:

```sh
PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE='/absolute/path/to/playwright/index.mjs' node scripts/check-games.mjs
```

The check targets the building, the only office, without a layout selector. It creates a disposable database and HOME, explicitly sets `HERDR_SOCKET_PATH=/nonexistent` and `HERDR_BIN_PATH=/usr/bin/false`, runs a scratch HTTP office on a free port other than 4870, and supplies twenty-three idle agents without herdr or account reads. It uses headless Chromium only, checks default and legacy-storage opening without a selector or founder desk, garden occupancy, front-door walking, moving balls / three landed darts / score visibility / immediate working-state departure, and closes the browser, HTTP server and database in `finally`. Screenshots default to `~/.review-inbox/handoffs/agent-office/games/` (override with `GAMES_SCREENSHOTS`). No live inbox state is read or changed.

## Gym

The south-east corner's square holds a gym along its outer wall, behind the reading nook's sofa, so it stays out of the opening views. It has a rubber floor, a lifting platform, a squat stand, a flat bench with uprights, a pull-up rig and a plate tree. `furnishGym` adds the boxes to the building's instanced furniture. `Gym.tsx` draws the bars and plates on shared geometry and materials.

`gym.ts` chooses lifters after the games. Eligibility follows the same rules, and nobody plays and trains at once. At most a third of the eligible agents train, and only one agent uses each station. Lifters stay in the gym while they are eligible. Newcomers are ordered by a hash of their id, and each takes the first free station starting from a hashed preference. Each lifter keeps a place in the gym's round (`GymSpot.slot`), and every visit (a minute on the office's clock, `visitAt`, the same as the regulars') they all move on a station together by the regulars' seeded step (`shiftAt`), so nobody does the same station twice running and everyone goes round all four. They walk between stations along the gym's lane (`cornerWalk`), not out through the office. The lifts are keyframed in body units and eased. Each station has one or two exercises:
- the platform: snatches and clean and jerks, done as singles;
- the squat stand: back squats and front squats (stood further back, so the bar comes off the hooks onto the front of the shoulders);
- the bench: bench presses;
- the pull-up rig: pull-ups and hanging knee raises.

A lifter does a routine seeded by who they are (`routine`). It is three sets, and on the platform a set is two or three singles. Where the station has two exercises, the routine changes over after the first or second set. Each set is followed by a rest of 6 to 9.5 seconds. Each rest in a routine has a different gesture: getting their breath back with hands on hips, shaking the arms out, or a sip from a water bottle. At the bench, the lifter sits up on the end of it to rest. The bar is back where it rests and the hands are off it for the whole rest, and the gesture comes in after the set and goes before the next. Agents and regulars alike do a fresh routine each visit, seeded by who they are and the visit, and then move on. A set that would not be over before they leave becomes a rest, so nobody walks off mid-set.

`GymPlayback` starts a lifter's clock once they are at their station and stops it when they leave, the same way games do. They count as there only once they stand still at the place their spot names (`samePlace`, by position, as the plan makes new spot objects each time it looks again), so moving round never starts a clock for one frame at the station they are about to walk to, and a fresh plan never restarts a set. The office looks again at each visit's end on a timer of its own. It records each lifter's station, seed and leaving time, and the bars go by it. A bar is lifted by whoever is training at its station, agent or regular. The avatar's rig has knees, ankles and elbows. A two-bone reach keeps the hands on the bar, and the bar follows the pose. Only a moving lift promotes the pacer; a rest's gesture asks only for the ambling rate.

`test/gym.test.ts` covers station choice, moving round the stations every visit (together, never the same twice running, nobody moved mid-visit), the clock and who is at each station, routines (sets, exercises, rest lengths and gestures, variety across lifters), rests (hands off, bar at rest, no jump into or out of them, sitting on the bench), never leaving mid-set, lift continuity, hands on the bar across heights and builds, and clear walking routes. The browser check (`npm run build`, then `PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE=… node test/gym.browser.mjs`) runs `test/gym-office.fixture.ts` through the scratch launcher on a free port, with herdr and the integrations off. It holds the clock at each lift's phases and at each station's first rest, using each lifter's own routine, and measures hand-to-bar distance in the scene. It takes desktop and phone screenshots and records a video, then gives everyone work and checks that the lifters leave. Output goes to `~/.review-inbox/handoffs/agent-office/gym/` (override with `SHOTS`).

## Ping pong

The south-west corner's square holds a full-size ping pong table along its outer wall, behind the reading nook's sofa, mirrored from the gym's corner (`cornerFrame` in `gym.ts`). `furnishPingPong` adds the top, lines, net and legs to the instanced furniture. `PingPong.tsx` draws the ball, plus a paddle in each player's right hand.

`pingpong.ts` chooses players after the games and the gym, with the same eligibility (`pingEligible`). Two agents play, paired by a hash of their id. If one player leaves, the other keeps their end and the next eligible agent takes the free one. An agent who would be alone plays a regular instead (below): when only one agent is idle at all they go to the table rather than to darts, and one left over after the games and the gym does the same. `PingPlayback` starts the rally once both players are at the table and stops it when either walks off. The match is baked once from a seed (`bakeRally`, a small seeded generator, no `Math.random`) and loops after its first points; `rallyTime` maps seconds since arrival onto it:
- **Arrival.** Both players ease from standing into their stance, and the first server steps in and picks the resting ball off the table with their free hand.
- **Between points.** The ball is caught in the free hand, brought in and held for a seeded pause of 0.6–1.5 s while both paddles go home; then it is tossed from that hand and served. Nothing snaps: the ball is always in flight, rolling or in a hand, and each player's step, lean and arms are eased keys.
- **Each point** varies its rally length (one shot to a dozen), speed, arc (now and then a lob), landing spot and where each player has to reach for it, stepping in or back and across, leaning and bending their knees for low balls. It ends in a miss (a late lunge, then the ball caught off the floor's side), a long ball past the far end, or a ball into the net that bounces back and rolls off the hitter's end into their hand. The catcher serves next.

Every flight is a true parabola under gravity, with bounce restitution of 0.75–0.9. Each paddle swings back, meets the ball and follows through. `playerPose` crouches each player, steps them in and across to the ball, reaches the blade and the free hand with the gym's two-bone reach and turns their head to follow the ball. Only a rally promotes the pacer.

`test/gym-pingpong.test.ts` covers pairing, the clock, who serves, bounce sides per ending, net clearance, the spread of rally lengths, speeds, arcs and landings, the seed, gravity, restitution, paddle contact, the ball's continuity and the held pause, joints without jumps, reach of blade and free hand for small and tall builds, and clear routes. The browser check (`PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE=… node test/gym-pingpong.browser.mjs`) uses the same scratch fixture as the gym's. It holds the clock at the serve, its bounces, the rally's hits, a point's end, the pause and the next toss, and a long and a net ball, and measures ball-to-blade distance and bounce positions in the scene. It takes desktop and phone screenshots and records videos, then checks that the players leave and the ball comes to rest while nobody plays, before the regulars take the table back. Output goes to `~/.review-inbox/handoffs/agent-office/gym/pingpong/`.

## Regulars

The gym and the table have regulars: six people from the neighbourhood (`regulars.ts`, drawn by `Regulars.tsx`) who use them whenever no agent does, so the corners are alive while everyone works. They exist only in the browser. There is no server state, no `world_agents` row and no herdr pane behind them. They are never in the plan's spots, so nothing that lists, counts, messages or answers agents sees them: not the projects panel, the header's agent count, Needs you or a team panel. Only the gym's bars and the table's rally see them beside the agents.

- **Who.** The cast is fixed: Ottilie, Kwabena, Marisol, Thandiwe, Brigitta and Ignatius. Each has a look derived from their id, with their own clothes, so the gym's bars still fit their height. Each has a home (a station or an end of the table), which the office's stage uses (`placeRegulars`, below).
- **Moving round.** Where they are comes from a timetable on the office's clock (`scheduleRegulars`). It is seeded, so it is the same in every browser, and the browser works it out again at each visit's end. Two regulars play at the table and four use the gym.
  - In the gym, everyone moves on every visit (a minute) round five roles: the four stations and a break. The step round is 1, 3 or -1 of five, seeded per visit, so nobody has the same role twice running.
  - The gym and the table are in opposite corners, 51 to 61 metres apart by the office's ways, so the table changes over more slowly. After each shift of five visits, one player hands over to someone from the gym and the other stays at their end. Over six shifts everyone plays.
  - In a shift's last visit the one taking over comes across and watches from beside the end they will take, so the changeover itself is 3 metres: a couple of seconds' walk and the ball's pick-up. If an agent has that end, they stay in the gym.
  - Whoever comes from the table takes the gym place of whoever went to it, so the others keep their order.
  - Inside a corner they walk up to its lane, along it and down (`cornerWalk`); between corners they take the office's routes.
- **Breaks and agents.** Nobody is ever at a station or an end an agent has, and never two at one place. A regular on a break, or whose station an agent has, goes to the mats (stretching, or sitting with their legs out and leaning back on their hands) or to the water cooler (sipping from a paper cup). When two or more are aside, the first two go to the cooler together, where they chat and high five. A regular whose end an agent has watches from across the table's lane. When everything is the agents', all six stand aside.
- **Partnering.** `partnerLone` puts a lone idle agent at the table (keeping the end they had). Whichever regular has the free end plays them, and the other table regular watches. The office's stage still puts each end's home regular there (`placeRegulars`), and `PingPlayback` keys the rally's clock by those ids, so whoever plays at that end reports their arrival under the home regular's id. Once a second agent is free, the agents go to the lounge games or the table together, and both table regulars are back at their ends.
- **Touches.** Between sets a lifting regular rests the same way agents do (above). After each point its winner punches the air with their paddle hand, and watchers cheer and follow the ball with their heads. The two at the cooler high five every fourteen seconds on the office's clock, sip from paper cups, and talk with a hand in between. The stretchers reach up, then fold to their toes.
- **Telling them apart.** A regular has no status lamp and a small light name pill with no project, harness or status line. Clicking one shows a one-line card ("Kwabena · Regular at the gym (not an agent)") with only a close button, never an agent's panel.
- **Cost.** They use the agents' rig (`Body`) and the shared lift and rally helpers (`liftRig` and `playRig` in `Avatar.tsx`). The cooler, mats, bottles and cups share one geometry and one material each, and a frame allocates nothing. Lifts and the rally promote the pacer as the agents' do. The small touches ask only for the ambling rate.

`test/regulars.test.ts` covers:
- the cast, the stage's homes, stepping aside and back along the lane, and partnering a lone agent;
- moving round: a new role every visit, three stations or more in a shift, every station, a break and a turn at the table over a morning, and the table changing over one player a shift, the next to play watching by their end the visit before (under 4 s to step in);
- for crowds from nobody to everything taken: one regular to a place, none where an agent is, every free end played and the cooler pair;
- that a walk across the gym (under 12 s) still leaves time for a first set in the visit;
- never being chosen as an agent, and the touches' timing.

The browser check (`npm run build`, then `PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE=… node test/regulars.browser.mjs`) runs four scratch offices from the gym fixture in turn: nobody idle, nobody idle again for about a minute across a visit's end, one idle agent and twenty-three. In each it checks who is where, that no regular is an avatar, in the count or in the projects panel, and that a click shows the card and no agent panel. The minute across a visit's end is recorded in real time and checks that the gym's regulars move on and rest between sets. It takes screenshots and videos into `~/.review-inbox/handoffs/agent-office/regulars/` (override with `SHOTS`).
